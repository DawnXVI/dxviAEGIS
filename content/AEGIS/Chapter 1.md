### Loria, 20XX

The sun crests the horizon, its amber glow shining over the land, bathing it in an incredibly pleasant warmth. The Kingdom of Loria awakens beneath the radiant light, cities quickly filling with noise as denizens spill out onto the streets, their days off to a strong start. Vendors, blacksmiths, taverns—all part of the hustle and bustle.

The sunlight shines, perhaps most elegantly, upon the Loria’s capital, Edenburth.

A tall, heavily guarded fence frames the perimeter of the city's center, a long stretch of land filled with lush greenery lying just past the gates. Atop a steep hill, the city’s most imposing structure stands tall, unscathed for centuries.

The castle of **King Rodin.**

Someone knocks on the large doors to the grand hall. A long, purple carpet runs along the floor in front of them, leading up to a short flight of stairs. Atop, two thrones, made with gold and the finest marble available.

His Majesty sits on the right, though the left remains conspicuously empty.

“Enter.”

His authoritative voice booms throughout the hall without him even trying to be loud. The doors soon open, revealing a woman with short auburn hair and feline ears atop her head. She walks inside, a two-handed greatsword mounted on her back.

The torches adorning the hall crackle faintly as she makes her way along the carpet. Her ears twitch subtly with each audible snap and pop from the flames.

Once she reaches the base of the staircase, she crouches to one knee, lowering her head with reverence.

“Your Majesty. The expedition team has set off to Gumera Cavern, as requested.”

Rodin leans back slightly, nodding in approval.

“Good. And what of the search?”

The woman’s stoicism falters for just a second. Unease creeps upon her back before she steels herself.

“...Still nothing… sire,” she admits hesitantly.

The King’s brow furrows. His fists visibly clench for but a moment before relaxing, his immense frustration evident.

“Why is it that my army’s *best* soldiers haven’t been able to locate **ONE** lone demon? What more resources must I give you all before you bring me results?!”

The woman makes no move to raise her head. Her gaze stays pointed straight at the floor.

“My deepest apologies, Your Majesty… We just... don't have any good leads. The only conclusion we’ve come to is that *someone* is harboring the demon, but we've had no luck  narrowing down suspects…”

“Then be more *direct!*" Rodin snaps back. "Have inspectors *visit homes*, or... *interrogate civilians* if you have to!"

He crosses his arms, his teeth grinding as he seems to contemplate something for a long moment.

He doesn't seem to realize the sorts of measures he's demanding.

After a short pause, the woman speaks, finally raising her head.

“With... all due respect, sire… It has been six years since this search began. We know the demon must feast frequently to survive, but there has been virtually no increase in disappearances or mysterious deaths across the cities since then. Isn’t it highly likely that the demon has left the ki-”

**"NONSENSE!!"**

Rodin interrupts her, his angered voice booming so loud, the woman's sensitive ears recoil from the sharp rise in volume.

She immediately lowers her head again, her expression unchanging, though her ears flatten the slightest bit against her head.

“...Forgive me... Your Majesty.”

Rodin regains his composure, taking a deep breath…

“Listen, Solanna... Demons are cunning. Insatiable. They don’t just ‘leave’ an area they’ve already infested. They’re like **parasites**—they hunt and feast, leeching off the lifeblood of a hearty city, no matter what it takes... The war taught us as much,” he says, leaning back against his throne with crossed arms, and closing his eyes.

“They know how to bide their time… How to make others wonder if they ever existed, before coming back to wreak havoc when it’s least expected.”

He slams his right fist on one of his throne's armrests.

“I will **NOT** be the king who sits idly by while a demon exists among his people with every intention of *feasting* upon their ***souls***,” he declares, his words spilling out with an almost chilling finality.

The woman wants to object. She's unsure. 

Unnerved.

*Unconvinced.*

But she would rather not risk stoking the flames of Rodin's clearly displeased soul.

“Of course... Your Majesty.”

Silence falls over the hall for another moment. 

“...One last question… if I may.”

"Go on…" says Rodin, raising an eyebrow.

The woman raises her head once more, this time fully making eye contact with Rodin.

“Gumera Cavern was sealed off centuries ago, during the war. Why the sudden decision to reopen it? It's, by all accounts, nothing but a large, empty space. Nothing of worth... No valuable ore... nothing more than a home to ravenous beasts."

Rodin sighs through his nose, closing his eyes with a thoughtful look, before giving his answer.

“...Gumera Cavern was turned into an underground stronghold by demons during the war. It wasn’t impenetrable by any means, but they had somehow taken control of the creatures within it, using them as their minions."

His recount of the event is spoken as though he was there himself.

"The battle to take the cavern was brutal... but a victory nonetheless. Once the demons were eradicated, the cavern was sealed, both trapping any remaining creature down there, and ensuring that it wouldn’t be used as a base by the enemy again.”

He opens his eyes, leaning forward slightly, his brow furrowing once more—this time with concern.

“The entrance to the cavern itself is near a path merchants often travel to get to Ravenhallow. And recently… We’ve gotten reports of... *sounds* coming from it. Strange, isn’t it? There are multiple layers of hard rock; several inches thick, all fused to the walls of the cavern. Sound shouldn’t at all be able to escape.”

He takes a deep breath and crosses his arms.

“Based on this information, I’ve made the decision to have the cave unsealed and cleared out for good. Better to get rid of the problem before it can become a catastrophe.”

The woman's eyes widen slightly, her own concerns bubbling up to the surface.

“Ah... So *that* is why you sent Aisha and my sister with them?”

"Precisely," says Rodin with a light nod. "Aisha’s raw power and Leilanna's ruthless flames are more than enough to exterminate whatever ruthless beasts remain within the cavern. I would have sent *you* along with your sister instead, but... I do still need one of you here to look after Lysandra.”

Solanna's thoughts wander to the two knights. Leilanna and Aisha are one-woman armies in their own right. Leilanna more so than Aisha—that girl is still relatively inexperienced—but there can be no doubt that the two can be an absurdly powerful force... Even despite their frequent 'disagreements.'

“I see… Very well, then. I shall return to my post,” she says.

Rodin nods. Solanna stands up, turning around and walking back the way she came. The double doors shut as she makes her exit.

Rodin maintains his stoicism only for another moment before lowering his head, his brow furrowing once more as he grits his teeth and clenches his fists. He brings one hand up, grabbing his head with an angry, almost desperate look.

“Damn it…" he hisses quietly to himself.

### Meanwhile, near Ravenhallow...

Hikari lies in a void blacker than the empty space between stars. Nothing but the sound of dripping water and a low, rumbling ambiance surrounds him.

Soon enough, he awakes.

Already bewildered, he sits up. He can’t see his surroundings at all. His eyes start hurting as they strain themselves trying to take in any light they can.

Where is he? Why is it so dark? Why is it so *cold?*

The only thing he can remember is his study session. Everything after that is fuzzy. His thoughts run wild as he tries his hardest to get his bearings. There’s a cold wetness on his back. It’s only when he stands up that he realizes that he’s woken up in a shallow puddle of water.

He looks around. Or rather, *tries* to.

The darkness is suffocating. Perhaps it’s his fear and unease kicking in, but each breath he takes feels like a genuine struggle to get air into his lungs. It’s as if the darkness is not only suppressing any trace of light, but the very **atmosphere** within it.

His eyes are still aching. He reaches out, trying to find something—anything—to grab onto.

He feels something. Something hard, weirdly smooth, and moist. A rock, perhaps?

He gathers the courage to call out into the darkness.

*"H-Hello?!"*

...

No answer. His voice reverberates loudly throughout the space, hurting his ears.

It appears he’s in a dark, dank cave.

He thinks he must be having some sort of nightmare. The atmosphere alone is too unsettling to be real.

Then…

He hears the hollow whistle of wind.

A breeze blows past him.

The cave trembles.

Hikari freezes. His body releases adrenaline into his system almost all at once. He can’t bring himself to turn around. After trying his hardest to steel himself even slightly, he slowly turns his head back toward the direction the wind is coming from, hoping to see something in the blackness.

His blood runs cold.

The darkness is growling at him.

### Just outside the cavern…

The expedition team approaches the entrance to Gumera Cavern, taking note of the overgrowth around its exterior.

One woman at the front of the team—a woman with short auburn hair, cat ears atop her head, and a perpetually irritated glare—steps forward.

Leilanna.

She has a dagger holstered on both hips, and her tail flicks with sharp movements. Her eyes scan the rock blocking the entrance for a moment before she crosses her arms and steps away, gesturing to a few of the Earth mages in the team.

“Alright. Hurry up and get to work,” she commands.

Three of the mages nod and step forward, standing in a triangular formation around the entrance. In a smooth motion, they thrust their arms forward, their hands opening with their palms pointed at the rock blocking the entrance. An ethereal, glowing light flares from their hands.

A faint rumbling soon emanates from the entrance. The earth beneath the team starts to quake ever so slightly. A young woman stands just a few feet to the left of Leilanna, wearing a green cowl and light armor, and sporting black rabbit ears atop her head.

Aisha.

She is the first to feel the ground shake beneath her.

“Whoa…” she utters in awe, gazing as the rock starts to shift.

The ground continues to tremble. After mere seconds, the first rock layer cracks. It recedes into the walls of the cavern entrance, its movements extremely rigid as it melds back into the earth.

The second rock layer is quicker to recede. The entrance expands in size as more layers of rock are removed.

Aisha puts a hand on her hip, furrowing her brow and letting out a light sigh.

“Man… What I would give to be able to do something like that…” she mutters enviously under her breath.

Leilanna's sensitive ears catch Aisha's words. She shoots a glance at her, raising an eyebrow and narrowing her eyes.

“Is it not enough that you could obliterate all of this rock with your own two feet?”

Aisha looks at Leilanna, unsurprised that she was heard. She merely shrugs, lightly tapping the ground with her foot.

“Well, sure, it is, but... It’d be nice to have a break from having to get physical for a change… My legs *do* get tired, you know,” she says, her response a bit too casual for Leilanna's liking.

“Right…” says Leilanna, simply turning her gaze back to the shifting rock with a barely concealed exasperation. Sometimes she can't tell if Aisha is too naive, too carefree, or both.

The mages continue their masterful work. Each rock layer cracks and recedes one after the other, until finally exposing the group of tightly packed boulders–the next obstacle to get through. The mouth of the cavern has opened up more, the entrance increasing in size.

Just as the boulders are exposed, Aisha's ears twitch. Her eyes widen the slightest bit as she freezes, turning her head a few degrees to the right and angling her left ear towards the cavern.

Leilanna notices the shift in her posture and glances in her direction once again.

“Something wrong?” she asks, suspicion creeping up her back as she instinctively moves her hands close to her daggers.

Aisha says nothing. As one boulder is broken apart, and then another, her ears twitch again. She can hear something. From deep, deep within the cavern.

Screaming.

She gasps.

“There’s someone down there!!” she yells out with urgency.

L'eiana's eyes widen in shock. “What?!”

Aisha turns to one of the expedition members behind her. “I’m going down there! Give me a light stone, **NOW!**”

The sudden authority in Aisha's voice startles the man as he momentarily fumbles with his satchel before pulling out a small, white stone and tossing it to her. She catches it before dashing toward the cavern’s entrance, the Earth mages stumbling out of the way.

L’eiana steps forward, equally taken aback by the sudden urgency of Aisha's voice and movements.

“Hold on! Aisha, are you sure?! What did you hear?!”

Aisha raises her leg, her muscles tensing up, tendons tightening like coiled springs. She glances back at L'eiana, her eyes gleaming with determination, and, perhaps, a little bit of arrogance. “Damn it, you have ears, don’t you?! OPEN THEM!”

L'eiana recoils, one eye twitching. The ***nerve*** of this girl! 

“Mind your tongue, you DISRESPECTFUL **LITTLE—!!**”

Aisha’s heel meets the mass of stone as she axe-kicks it hard, the loud boom cutting L’eiana’s reprimand short. In an instant, most of the rock caves inward, cracking all over. Aisha kicks it again. This time, it shatters. The entire cavern trembles as the pieces of rock cascade down into the larger space.

A wave of pain shoots up her leg. She doesn’t usually kick anything with such force, let alone a huge chunk of stone. Still, it hardly fazes her. She sprints down into the dark cavern, eyes still gleaming with that determined shine.

The stone in her hand starts giving off a bright light that penetrates through the darkness effortlessly, even as she goes deeper and deeper.

L’eiana huffs with frustration, standing up straight before turning to the expedition team.

“Alright, change of plans! Into the cavern! Hurry up!”

She runs into the abyss, the team and Earth mages following not far behind. What was supposed to be an extermination operation has turned into a search-and-rescue. At the very least... They're prepared.

For what is waiting down there; something ancient and very, very hungry…

They have to be.

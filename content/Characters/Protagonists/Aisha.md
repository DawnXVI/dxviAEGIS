## Overview

Aisha, as it stands, is one of the most powerful knights on the entire planet. Not in terms of magic, no. That power is *all* physical. 

Aisha stands at about an average height for a female Lapin. Her skin is a dark, chocolate-colored tone that pairs well with the bright lavender of her eyes, which always seem to shimmer with a fierce determination. Her hair is black, neck-length, and lightly messy (a deliberate styling choice; she thinks it looks cute this way), and atop her head stand tall, fluffy, black rabbit ears. These ears are very highly sensitive to sound, and are always twitching and swiveling in response to noise and to express emotion. She has a round black rabbit tail on her lower back, but her ears are the most noticeable indicator of her being a demi-human. 

As a knight in [[Characters/Antagonists/King Rodin XXVII\|Rodin]]'s army, she's almost always seen wearing her armor, which, for the sake of mobility, consists mostly of hardened leather. She also wears tall boots with steel plating on the front and on the outsoles, as these are the areas that see the most physical impact during combat (she's a rabbit girl; she does a ***lot*** of kicking). This steel is enchanted to be more durable, as it would become deformed over time from the force of her kicks otherwise. Aisha also wears gauntlets with steel plating over the forearms, which mainly serve a defensive purpose: blocking. This is about as much metal as she can have on her body before she starts to slow down.

Aisha is also always seen with a satchel. This satchel is enhanced with a spatial enchantment that makes the inside much bigger than the actual size of the satchel. With how much she needs to eat, this enchantment is a necessity for all the snacks Aisha carries with her constantly.

## Background

Strange circumstances led to the complete disappearance (and assumed death) of the entire Lapin race... Save for Aisha. She lost her parents in this disaster, and she was merely an infant; too young to form any memories of them. That same incident led to her being taken in and adopted by [[Characters/Deuteragonists/Gunther]], a wise and retired old wizard who raised her in Edenburth, the capital of Loria.

As a young girl, Aisha always got quite a bit of attention from others, and this was in large part due to her seemingly being the last Lapin left. Her natural strength was always something that drew awe from those around her, and she grew up being constantly praised by her peers and adults she interacted with. Though this would've normally caused a girl like her to develop a strong sense of narcissism and entitlement, Gunther's fantastic work as a father prevented that from happening. Seeing the potential she had, he did his best to raise her with heroic values, mostly succeeding in turning her into a kind, well-meaning warrior. While *some* level of ego remained, it wasn't at all enough to negatively impact her social life or her attitude towards innocents.

Much of Aisha's childhood was spent helping others, which Gunther taught her was the right thing to do. It was also how she initially got so much attention. Being the only Lapin in the kingdom was one thing, but the true value in that was only realized once her good deeds started, and the true reason she began doing this in the first place was because of her admiration for the knights of Loria. She saw how dedicated some of them were to the people and their safety, and she loved the idea of being like one of them. Gunther, not one to deny her of her dreams, felt it was a good idea to start training Aisha, both to help her get stronger and to help her learn the discipline needed to be a knight.

Needless to say, Aisha quickly gained the respect of many people across Edenburth. Not a day went by that she wasn't out and about helping people with whatever they needed; be it a physically demanding task or something entirely mundane and simple. [[Characters/Antagonists/King Rodin XXVII\|Rodin]] noticed early on the respect Aisha was garnering and kept a close eye on her. It didn't take him long to recognize the opportunity in front of him. Seemingly the only Lapin left on the planet, an official asset to his army? The idea was too good to pass up. Thus, when Aisha was 13, Rodin sent [[Characters/Tritagonists/Leilanna]] to Gunther's home to pass a message along to them: Rodin wanted to have Aisha put through training to become an official knight in his army. Needless to say, Aisha was ecstatic. So much so, she was literally bouncing off the walls out of excitement for a good 5 minutes before calming down...

It was soon after her training started that she met [[Characters/Tritagonists/Lysandra]], and the two hit it off very well. Their friendship blossomed from then on, and after 3 full years of training, Aisha was officially knighted at the age of 16. 2 more years have passed since then, and Aisha continues to serve Rodin and the people  as a highly respected knight. Just last year when she was 17, she was gifted land by Rodin, and currently lives there in her own comfy two-story house that she, amazingly enough, built with her own two hands.

## Personality

Aisha is a *sweet*, *smug*, and, at times, *arrogant* young woman. While she would gladly help you carry some heavy cargo or set up your shop for the day, she would also just stand there and let you ramble on and on about something you're *clearly* wrong about, just so she can rub it in your face once you realize what a fool you are.

As is normal for a young Lapin woman like her, Aisha is very strongly emotional and can get a little hyper with any level of excitement she feels. She envies those who are able to use magic, and often becomes starry-eyed and awe-struck at displays of magical power from her comrades. She's just very bubbly in general.

Aisha is also quite quick to anger, and will fiercely defend herself or her friends if anyone starts to get aggressive with them. Aisha, as nice as she is, has little patience for the unsavory few who show themselves to be incredibly rude, especially if their behavior becomes targeted towards those she cares about. 

That being said, Aisha *does* believe in second chances. She's more than willing to try to talk things out with an aggressor and try to come to a peaceful resolution, or at least help them with whatever problem they're having that's causing them to be such a jerk. If the aggressor shows themselves to be a more cruel bastard than Aisha initially anticipated, she won't even bother with trying to be nice.

Aisha otherwise shows a great deal of kindness to the people who are just going about their days and living normally. A lot of people across Edenburth genuinely like her, and her job as a warrior knight in Rodin's army has given her opportunities to visit other Lorian cities and build a positive reputation with the citizens of those areas as well. Nothing has changed in terms of how she goes about her days even after becoming a knight; she still helps people with whatever she can when the need arises. She doesn't like being taken advantage of, however. Her bubbliness doesn't make her ditzy by any means. She has quite the knack for detecting when someone's being too greedy with her kindness, and she's not afraid to be stern about confronting these issues.

Aisha's best quality is her own selflessness. Not once since she was officially knighted has she ever hesitated to jump into danger to save someone's life. She knows that as a knight, her top priority must be the safety of the people she serves. And *boy,* does she take that to heart. She knows when to completely drop her bubbly attitude and get serious, and this shows most often when she goes into full "savior" mode. 

Aisha's cockiness is something most people don't see normally. That's because it doesn't show up in her behavior when interacting with people. Rather, this cockiness shows itself when she's in combat and eliminating a threat. Aisha takes pride in her strength and loves it when one hard kick or two puts down a giant, hostile creature that would have otherwise killed an innocent person. Both the act of saving someone's life and seeing her own strength in action provides a sense of great satisfaction to Aisha that leaves her feeling very confident in herself.

## Friends & Family

### Gunther

[[Characters/Deuteragonists/Gunther]] is Aisha's adoptive father, though if you asked Aisha, "adoptive" would undermine the emotional connection she has with him. She's more than happy to call him "dad," and truly views him as such despite him not being a Lapin.

Aisha is fully aware of how difficult it must've been for Gunther to raise her because of just how *hyper* she was (and still is) as a child, but she's incredibly grateful for the patience and kindness he showed her regardless, and loves him just as strongly as she would her real father. Gunther has always been Aisha's main emotional support, and still is even today. 

Though Aisha is often busy thanks to her knightly duties, she still tries to make time to visit Gunther at his shop, and she'll happily talk to him about whatever's on her mind, whether it be someone whose life she saved, an argument she got into with [[Characters/Tritagonists/Leilanna]] (for the millionth time), or just something random she finds interesting. To this day, she still goes to Gunther for help and advice on anything she's feeling unsure/nervous about. 

The truth is that whenever Aisha's with Gunther, she melts right back into that little girl she used to be. She still loves to be patted on the head by him like he always did when she was small (though she'd be embarrassed to admit this to anyone). She's, for lack of a better term, a Daddy's Girl.

## Strengths

### Sheer Might

In a magic-centered world, you'd think the inability of Lapins to use magic would make them weak. But that's not the case. Lapins were the single most powerful demi-human race on the planet in terms of physical strength, and it's *because* of this power that they couldn't use magic. Their strength was more than enough for them to survive in this world; thus, Mother Nature deemed it a fair trade-off for them to lack any magic capability.

Aisha, as a Lapin herself (and the last remaining one), is a living representative of this fames strength. As a child, she was already naturally stronger than most other kids, but it was the training she received from Gunther, and then the subsequent training she received as a knight, that allowed her to push that strength to further heights. 

As of now, Aisha's kicks are *devastating.* Lapins were always famous for their leg strength; in fact, the most powerful Lapin in history was capable of levelling fortress walls with her kicks. Though Aisha isn't anywhere near at that level of power, by the standards of most kingdoms, she still may as well be a walking siege weapon. A few full force kicks from her can break through incredibly hard rock, or shatter several bones at once. Aisha herself has rarely ever had to kick this hard, and she'd prefer not to use such extreme, overly lethal force either way, as this would almost certainly end up hurting her own body. Still, even when she's holding back, her kicks are nothing to scoff at. 

Though Aisha's leg strength is her greatest physical attribute, she certainly doesn't skip arm day either. She's able to lift extremely heavy objects that most people can't. As a Lapin who began training at a very young age and has received formal knight training, her limit is about 1,900 lbs (860 kg), though adrenaline can easily push her past that limit (though, not without serious muscle injury). Ironically, because her arm strength is already enough in most cases, she normally defaults to using punches when in a fight rather than kicking, which she reserves more for large creatures that would require much more force to take care of.

### Agility

Naturally, Aisha is as fast and agile as she is strong. Her short, explosive bursts of speed allow her to traverse a lot of distance very quickly, and this also makes her able to close the distance between her and her opponent before they're able to gather the mana for a spell. This is a tactic she relies heavily on as someone who ends fights quickly. Get close, don't let them use their magic, knock them out.

### Culinary Prowess

Because of how much Aisha eats, she's learned to appreciate good food. She's learned to *make* good food, even. Though it's a facet of her skillset that not many people see in person, it's nonetheless an extremely important skill for her to have. Aisha cooks ***damn*** good, nutrient-dense meals that would have anyone's mouth watering.

## Weaknesses

### Endurance... Or lack thereof.

With Lapins being so incredibly strong, it's only logical that their biology is extremely demanding in terms of energy. One of the most important aspects of biology is the simple fact that *their adrenal systems are hyperactive,* and this is what allows for the immediate, explosive reactions they were capable of. However, it also means they burn through stamina quickly, and aren't built for long fights. This very much applies to Aisha. Even at rest, her body is always alert and burning through more energy than the average person. After combat, she's almost always winded and needs rest.

This *also* means that she needs to eat a ***lot.*** Her body's energy demands are insane compared to that of a human. Huge meals that could feed multiple people are normal for her, though she'd personally prefer that no one actually watch her eat these meals, mainly out of... Well, she just doesn't want anyone thinking she's some kind of un-ladylike, ill-mannered *slob.*

Still. Because of her body's demands, she'll always be carrying a lot of snacks in her satchel for quick, on-the-go calories. 

### Complacency

Gunther's made a grand effort to keep all the praise Aisha has received from turning her into an entitled egotist. While he's been largely successful, there's only so much he can do as one man raising a hyperactive bunny girl, and there are things he knows he should let Aisha learn herself.

Aisha has been getting more and more complacent with her current skillset and strength. Her pride and cockiness are beginning to become a detriment to her. Because of how strong she is now, she feels she doesn't need to train much anymore, and that her current endurance problems aren't much of an issue considering she almost always ends conflicts in one kick. She already tends to be carefree at times, so this complacency is something she'll have to learn the *hard* way is only going to be detrimental to her in the long run.

### Loneliness

Though it'd be impossible to tell at a glance, Aisha struggles internally with feelings of loneliness. She's never seen another one of her kind. She is, as many of Loria's citizens believe, the last remaining Lapin. She experiences profound sadness when she sees a happy family all of the same race. She wants that connection. She wants to meet *her people.* She simply *refuses* to accept that all the other Lapins are just *gone*. She strongly believes that there *has* to be at least one other Lapin *somewhere.* She wants to know what happened to them. Where the remaining few are, if there *are* any left. 

### Durability

As a Lapin, Aisha depends heavily on mobility in combat. She has to move quickly and hit hard and fast, and this is what necessitates the lightweight armor she wears. She can't afford any cut to her speed. Her dense muscle and sturdy skeleton allow her to take a good amount of blunt force trauma, but because she has no magic capability, she's extremely vulnerable to any type of offensive magic. The smallest, weakest fireball can scorch her skin, and any spell of moderate complexity can be enough to put her down if she's hit. This effectively makes her a *glass cannon.* She hits hard enough to end fights in only a couple blows, but if her opponent's magic hits her once, or if her opponent just keeps her at a distance long enough for her to become exhausted, it's game over.

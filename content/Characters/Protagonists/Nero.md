## Overview

Nero is what you could consider a "back-alley dweller" who steals from innocent people not to survive, but to live comfortably. He is a very short scrawny boy with brown curly hair, a stubble and orange eyes. Typical of a criminal living on the streets, you will almost never see him without some dirt on his face or clothes, which are visibly worn down and patched up in several places.

***
## Background

Living in an orphanage since before he could form conscient thought, Nero always romantisized the criminal life as a child due to thinking it was "cool". Because of this, he never got along with the other kids with the exception of Venn (insert hyperlink here later). He wanted to be a criminal so much, one night, at the age of 7, he decided to pack up the little stuff he had and ran away from the orphanage. 

Unfortunately, after living off pickpockets for about a week, reality was quite a lot more bleak than he envisioned it. However, being too scared to come back to the orphanage in fear of being imprisoned, he decided to suck it up and continue. At a certain point in his life, he realized he no longer had to steal to live a nice life, but the excitement and adrenaline he got from pulling robberies off was too good for him to pass on. He continues roaming the alleyways and shady areas looking for unsuspecting passerbys to satiate his kleptomania.

***
## Personality

Nero is a *smug,* *cheeky,* and *slightly arrogant* bastard that will not refuse to laugh at your misfortunes in your face. Despite the moral reprecussions of his line of work, he takes pride in what he does and is constantly searching for ways to improve his "craft". He tends to go with the flow of whatever is happening around him unless it will lead to an unfavorable situation to him or has the potential to get him what he wants. 

He DOES favor the people who are nice to him even after his initial jerk behavior, gradually softening up to the point of friendly banter and sarcastic jokes. Due to the nature of his everyday relationships, he gets protective of people he is friends with as to not lose the few that he has.

## Abilities

### Physical Enhancement
It is no wonder that being a criminal involves getting rowdy with the guards or potential competitors, because of which Nero is proficient in physical enhancement, mostly to run away or knock out the person he's engaging with. However, pickpocketing being Nero's main source of income, after being caught one too many times, he thought of a way to practically guarantee he would be quick enough to pull them off. He decided to "overclock" his brain's capabilites for information transmission alongside his usual bodily enhancements, effectively making him percieve time several times slower than normal people. Unfortunately, making your brain work several times faster for a small period of time tends to make the user's head throb and their nose bleed. In the end though, Nero decided that the pros of this spell outweigh the cons.

### Time Manipulation
After years of sneaky life Nero obtained a near perfect sense of rhythm due to stealth requiring impeccable timing. This resulted in Nero using his proficiency to learn time manipulation magic, which requires great understanding of the flow of time to pull off. His abilities include speeding up time on himself, effectively making him faster, slowing down time on his enemies to make them slower, "locking" things in place and accumulating force on them with actions, then releasing the "lock", applying the accumulated force all at once.

### Combat Skill
Unlike royal guards or adventurers, Nero's fighting style is reliant not on his own skill, but dirty tricks and distractions. He will blind, deafen, disorient and abuse the critical points of his opponents to claim victory.

### Dexterity
Nero's constant involvement in stealth and his natural dexterity given to him by his nature of a mouse demi-human resulted in him obtaining an insanely flexible and precise body, capable of weaving attacks, dodging ranged spells and climbing through most tight spaces without difficulty.

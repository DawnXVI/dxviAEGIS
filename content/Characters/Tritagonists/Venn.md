## Overview

Venn is your run-of-the-mill crook working for a bigger organization to make a living through questionable sources, although his proficiency in diplomacy places him above low class grunts. His usual assignments entail selling off procured goods at exorbitant prices to clueless customers, negotiating business with potential collaborators, but also occasionally roughening up the unlucky souls misfortunate enough to anger his boss.

Venn's attire consists of leather pants, a turtleneck underneath a long coat, and a shoulder bag for the items he sells. He has middle length black hair and jade colored eyes. He generally tries to keep himself stylish and tidy to look professional and appealing, but working as a criminal dealmaker doesn't always allow you to be the cleanest. 

***
## Background

Abandoned at an orphanage since his birth because of his parents just not being able to support a child, Venn lived his days talking to and playing with [[Characters/Protagonists/Nero]] until his eventual departure, after which Venn tried to talk to the other kids but got rejected by everyone due to his built-up bad reputation. Despite encouraging [[Characters/Protagonists/Nero]] to follow the life of crime out of naiveness, Venn himself was too afraid to leave the safety of the orphanage. 

After reaching the age of 13, he started taking up side-gigs to earn cash to hopefully live on his own someday. One of those side-gigs just happened to be selling stuff at a shop ran by a shady guy. One day, at his job, a group of gangsters came into the building and required to see his boss. Venn, realizing that these people were NOT friendly and could potentially lose him his job, weaved the conversation while activating his call stone to alert the nearby knights to his predicament. After the fact, Venn's boss, having recieved the report from him, decided to introduce Venn to the person that provided the items the shopkeeper sold, as he was impressed by Venn's ability to lead the conversation. The "Dealer" proposed a contract to Venn; he would recieve items and sell them, give up a percentage of sales and pocket the rest. Venn, doing some quick calculations, came to the conclusion that this arrangement would net him more than thrice the cash he was recieving currently, and accepted the deal without a second thought. However, being the inexperienced teen that he was, he didnt't realize that he had just tied a noose around his own neck.

He continues peddling around the cities, searching for a way to somehow, someway break away from the conned life he is living and make peace with the regret of encouraging another person to take the same path as him.

***
## Personality

Venn is a *cunning* and *slithery* merchant who will masterfully lead you on and force you to buy stuff that you would never even need. He doesn't want to do this, but has to in fear of getting culled like the many coworkers he had personally witnessed being "laid off". Despite being a criminal, he is surprisingly nice to strangers he doesn't deem criminals themselves (discerning which is no problem for a person with experience like Venn), even going so far as to rarely donate to charities to "atone for his sins".

Unlike civilians (not counting buyers), Venn, ironically enough, despises any and all bandits, crooks, swindlers and the like but most importantly loathes himself for not seeing through the scam that cost him his free life. He feels no guilt pulling wool over the eyes of criminals, actively thinking of ways he could disrupt the shady dealings he oversees without placing a target on his own back.

## Weaknesses

### Lingering regret
Venn constantly remembers his life in the orphanage, specifically [[Characters/Protagonists/Nero]], which makes him hesitate when interacting with innocent people in fear of condemning another person to his lifestyle. On particularly bad days, he sometimes freezes due to his guilt weighing too heavy on his shoulders, overthinking what could have happened to his one and only true friend.

## Overview

Venn is your run-of-the-mill crook working under a larger organization, making his living through means that are equal parts illegal and immoral. However, his proficiency in diplomacy places him above the average low-rank grunt. His usual assignments involve selling stolen goods at exorbitant prices to clueless customers, negotiating business with potential collaborators, *and* occasionally roughing up the poor souls who are foolish (or just unlucky) enough to anger his boss.

Venn is tall for a Naga his age, and quite handsome at that, which greatly aids him in being a charismatic swindler. He has tan skin typical of most Nagas, and his eyes are a shimmering jade green color with thin, slit-shaped pupils. His hair is a dull red color, neck-length and shaggy in appearance, which only adds to his charisma. As a python-type Naga, he doesn't have venomous fangs; rather, all of his teeth are normal length, but very sharp. Being a non-venomous Naga also means he has two functioning legs, with a long, thick snake tail protruding from his lower back, the scales of which are a brighter red than his hair.

His attire most commonly consists of leather pants, a turtleneck underneath a long coat, and a shoulder bag for the items he sells. As a guy who relies on natural charisma to rip people off, he does his best to keep himself looking stylish, tidy, professional, and appealing. Still, being a criminal dealmaker comes with times where it's difficult to stay clean.

## Background

Venn, like [[Characters/Protagonists/Nero]], was abandoned at the doorstep of the orphanage in Vannahal shortly after he was born. Though whereas *Nero* was sent away to spare him the pain of growing up in a troubled home, *Venn* was an accident—a child his parents genuinely couldn't afford to keep. That didn't make abandoning him painless. Leaving your infant son at an orphanage, even when you've convinced yourself it's the right call, is still abandonment. Venn's parents seemed to know that. They wrapped him in a large poncho before they left, a small gesture meant to tell him—when he was old enough to understand it—that what they did wasn't out of cruelty, and that they wished for him to be warm and taken care of. Venn would eventually pass this poncho on to Nero as a token of their brotherly bond.

Venn lived his early childhood years talking to and playing with Nero until the latter's eventual departure. Though Venn tried to talk to the other kids and be friends with them, he had already built up a terrible reputation due to his antics with Nero, and thus was unsurprisingly rejected by the lot. Despite having been the one to encourage Nero to leave and ultimately give him that final *push* that helped him make his decision, Venn himself was too afraid to do the same. It's something he still feels like a hypocrite about to this day. When days turned into weeks since Nero's departure, it became clear he wasn't coming back. Worrying that something terrible had happened to his best friend, Venn gathered the courage to leave the orphanage as well, in search of Nero.

Venn went through [[Characters/Protagonists/Nero#^1\|essentially the same thing Nero did.]] Though he was *initially* nervous to be out and alone by himself (unlike Nero), he still decided he could try to indulge in the criminal life he and Nero fantasized so much about while he was looking for him. It went about as well as you'd expect, and led to the *same* realization Nero had.

*Criminals go to jail when they're caught.*

Like Nero, Venn lasted as long as he did through luck and talent. Though whereas Nero's talent was stealth, *Venn's* talent was pure charisma. Even as a young boy, he had quite the knack for making people let their guard down, very quickly coming up with convincing lies, and making himself appear as though he's merely another innocent kid. Again, like Nero, this was purely for the sake of survival early on. But in Venn's case, he didn't develop kleptomania or anything like that. He became afflicted with something that's potentially even worse. He was *losing* his moral compass bit by bit with the time that passed. He slowly became more *okay* with what he was doing, becoming a little more *numb* to the pain of knowingly scamming innocent people and potentially ruining financial lives.

By the age of 13, Venn still held onto the hope that Nero was out there somewhere, but that hope was slowly beginning to dwindle. He began taking up side-gigs to earn cash in the hopes that he could some day live on his own away from all the trouble he's caused thus far. One of those side-gigs just happened to be a job he procured at a shop owned by someone *shady*. All he had to do was be the "salesboy" in charge of convincing customers to make purchases. One day, a group of men came into the building and asked to see the owner of the shop. Venn took one look at them and realized... these people were **not** friendly, and whatever they wanted with his boss couldn't have been good. He didn't care much for the old man; he could tell clear as day that the guy wasn't running some normal gift shop, but he *was* worried about losing his job. Venn turned on the charisma with a level of effort he hadn't used before, successfully managing to spin the conversation with the men in various directions before stealthily activating the call stone at the counter to alert nearby knights to his predicament. A group of them arrived just before things got violent, and the men were taken away.

Venn soon after told his boss about the incident, and the old man—greatly impressed by Venn's silver tongue—decided to introduce him to his... *Dealer.* The guy who provided the old man with the items sold at the shop. This "Dealer" made a proposition to Venn. Venn would receive items acquired through "questionable" means and sell them, giving up a percentage of sales to the "organization" the dealer is associated with and pocketing the rest for himself. Venn, after some quick calculations, came to the conclusion that this arrangement would net him more than thrice the cash he was already earning working at the shop, and accepted the deal without a second thought. However, being the inexperienced teen that he was, he didn't realize that by accepting that proposition, he had just tied a noose around his own neck. He had *joined a crime syndicate.*

Venn continues peddling to clueless customers around the city, having fully accepted that *this* is what he is and what he *will* be for the foreseeable future. His moral compass, though he hasn't completely lost it, is no longer a factor in determining his actions in the heat of the moment. Instead, the regret lives deep inside Venn, driving him to avoid witnessing the consequences of his actions to others—avoid taking responsibility for the pain he causes. He's completely given up his search for Nero, and now tries (futilely) to make peace with the guilt he feels for encouraging his only friend—his *brother*— to walk the terrible path of the criminal.

## Personality

Venn is a *cunning* and *slithery* merchant who could masterfully lead you on and convince you to buy something extraordinarily useless; something you would never even need. His morals always scream at him from deep within, but these screams continuously fall on deaf ears, as Venn has long since accepted this path he's taken. Not only that, but it's not like he *could* stop—he fears being culled like so many of his "coworkers" he's previously witnessed being “laid off”. Aside from that, Venn is surprisingly nice to innocents who don't happen to be current con targets of his, and Venn himself is quite good at discerning criminals from innocents.

Venn frequently (and anonymously) donates to charities. It's the one effort he feels he can afford to make to "atone" for his sins.

Venn, ironically enough, despises any and all bandits, crooks, swindlers and the like, ***especially*** those who seem to *take pleasure* in what they do. Though, Venn is noticeably much less judgmental towards those who, like him, do it to survive. He feels no guilt pulling wool over the eyes of criminals, and often fantasizes about dismantling the entire criminal underworld, or at least being able to disrupt the shady dealings the syndicate oversees.

## Abilities

### Charisma

Some might call it a form of hypnosis. Venn calls it his "venomous charm." The ability to talk your way out of most situations and convince people to do things for you has been a staple of Venn's arsenal since he was a child. And now, as an experienced con man, Venn's charisma has reached levels where he can convince some of the most skeptical individuals that a vial of colored ash mixed with the sweat off some fat drunkard's head is *real* unicorn blood. 

### Stealth

See, the *weird* thing about scamming people is... they don't really *like* being scammed. They tend to get *mad,* even. Thus, Venn has, on countless occasions, moved himself far from the reach of those he swindles to avoid the potential repercussions he would otherwise face, and this has helped him build up quite a lot of skill in stealth.

### Combat Skill

Venn knows that in his line of work, he has to get up close and personal, and that there *will* be situations where escape isn't an option. Thus, he's spent a good amount of time over the years learning to fight and defend himself from both physical and magical threats.

### Tail

Venn's tail is fully prehensile, as are the tails of all Nagas. While his tail is too thick to use as a fifth limb, it's heavy enough that he can use it as a weapon, most notably for *painful* tail whip strikes.

### Core Strength

Venn himself is quite fit, and his stature gives him a natural intimidation factor that helps him avoid some confrontations. Though where Venn's physicality *really* shines is his core strength, which is already naturally high for bipedal Nagas like him. This naturally high core strength is needed to support the weight of their tails, and Venn is no exception. But because of the training he already has in combat, he's firmly in the upper echelon of Naga core strength. This makes Venn's athletic performance exceptional, and he can generate a lot of force in both his upper and lower body, enabling him to move fast, stay hanging on ledges or bars for prolonged periods of time, and throw punches and kicks that *really* hurt.

## Weaknesses

### Regret

Venn lives with the crushing guilt of thinking he sent his best friend to a life of loneliness and pain, and after all this time without ever finding Nero, Venn firmly believes that he's *dead.* Venn used to cry himself to sleep on most nights thinking of his time at the orphanage and how much he misses Nero and being back there with him, but nowadays, he can hardly express his grief properly at all. On nights where the guilt bubbles up, he merely looks at the sky and... stares. The guilt of being a con man also weighs down on him, but it doesn't make him hesitate when he's in the middle of scamming somebody. The only reason for this, however, is that he *can't afford* to hesitate. Not with the noose around his neck that seems to tighten ever so slightly with each passing day.

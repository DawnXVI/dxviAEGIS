What if Earth was a place where [magic](Magic) *thrived;* a constant presence in the lives of humans everywhere?

What if the existence of magic itself changed the course of history, causing the medieval era to stick around, bringing the monarchies and political structures that existed back then into the modern age?

What if magic dictated the very forces of nature and evolution that led mankind to where it is today?

That's the very world this story is about.

Not our own, but not some arbitrary planet with a strange, otherworldly name either.

This <i>is</i> Earth.

An Earth whose story was written by magic.

An Earth whose story has crossed with the Earth we know and love.
